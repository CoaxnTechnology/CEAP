1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes guidelines to safeguard children from all forms of violence, exploitation, abuse, and neglect within the school premises and during school-related activities.

2. LEGAL BASIS
- Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012
- Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009 (RTE)
- Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015
- Karnataka State Child Protection Policy, 2020
- CBSE Guidelines on Child Safety and Protection

- Student Member: Class 10 representative ( girl student)

4. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
4.1 Chairperson
- Ensures policy implementation and annual review
- Coordinates with CPC and external agencies
- Escalates serious incidents to authorities

4.2 Child Protection Officer
- First point of contact for all safeguarding concerns
- Documents and reports incidents per POCSO guidelines
- Conducts capacity building programs annually
- Coordinates with Child Welfare Committee and Police Special Juvenile Police Unit

4.3 All Staff
- Maintain professional boundaries with students
- Report any suspicion of abuse immediately to CPO
- Complete mandatory safeguarding training every 2 years
- Follow Code of Conduct as specified in this policy

5. SAFEGUARDING MEASURES
- Visitor register maintained at entrance
- CCTV coverage in all common areas
- Staff background verification before appointment
- Criminal background checks for all outsourced personnel
- Suggestion/concern box available for students
- No student left unattended after school hours
- Bus attendant provided on all school buses

6. TRAINING
- All staff complete online safeguarding module (annual)
- CPO attends advanced child protection training biennially
- Students receive age-appropriate safety awareness sessions (termly)

7. REPORTING PROCEDURE
Step 1: Any staff member or student reports concern to CPO within 24 hours
Step 2: CPO documents the concern on the Incident Report Form (IRF-01)
Step 3: CPC reviews and decides on immediate action
Step 4: If criminal activity suspected, report to Police (Child Welfare Cell) within 24 hours as per POCSO Section 19
Step 5: Parents/guardians informed unless it would endanger the child
Step 6: Case reviewed until resolution; follow-up after 3 months

8. ZERO TOLERANCE
The school maintains zero tolerance for:
- Physical abuse, sexual abuse, emotional abuse
- Bullying (including cyberbullying)
- Discriminatory practices based on caste, religion, gender, disability
- Any form of corporal punishment

9. REVIEW
This policy is reviewed annually by the CPC and updated as needed.
