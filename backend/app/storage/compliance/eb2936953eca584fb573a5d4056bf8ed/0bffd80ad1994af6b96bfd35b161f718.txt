SCHOOL TRANSPORT STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)
Government Higher Secondary School, Chennai
Ref No: SOP-TRANS-2025/003 | Version: 2.0 | Effective: 01-Apr-2025

OBJECTIVE:
Ensure safe and efficient transportation of all students using school transport facilities.

SCOPE:
Applies to all school-owned buses, hired buses, contract vehicles, and vans used
for student transportation under school management.

1. VEHICLE SPECIFICATIONS
  - Colour: Golden Yellow as per AIS-063 standard
  - Signage: "School Bus" on front and rear, "On School Duty" on hired vehicles
  - School name and contact number visible on all vehicles
  - Vehicle fitness certificate valid and displayed
  - Insurance: Comprehensive third-party + personal accident cover
  - GPS tracker: Mandatory, real-time monitoring active
  - CCTV: Both interior and exterior, footage retained 30 days
  - Speed Governor: Capped at 40 km/h, non-bypassable
  - First-aid kit: Fully stocked, checked monthly
  - Fire extinguisher: One per vehicle, annual servicing
  - Horizontal grills on windows (no openable lower windows)
  - Emergency exit door on all vehicles

2. DRIVER REQUIREMENTS
  - Minimum 4 years commercial driving experience
  - Valid Heavy Motor Vehicle (HMV) driving license
  - No traffic violations in past 3 years (verified from traffic police clearance)
  - Medical fitness certificate renewed annually
  - School uniform worn while driving
  - Training: Annual defensive driving and first-aid refresher
  - Background verification: Police verification conducted annually

3. BUS ATTENDANT REQUIREMENTS
  - Trained in first-aid and emergency response
  - Female attendant mandatory for transport of younger students (Classes I-V)
  - Attendance register maintained for all pickups/drop-offs
  - Must verify student identity before allowing boarding/alighting
  - Mobile phone available for emergency communication only

4. ROUTE PLANNING
  - Routes planned by Transport Committee with parent representatives
  - Alternative routes identified for road closures/traffic congestion
  - Maximum travel time to school: 45 minutes (one way)
  - Pick-up and drop times communicated to parents 7 days in advance
  - No vehicle stops without prior approval

5. BOARDING AND ALIGHTING PROCEDURE
  - Students must be at bus stop 5 minutes before scheduled time
  - Bus attendant verifies student identity
  - Students board in an orderly manner, seated promptly
  - No standing while vehicle is in motion
  - Students may alight only at designated stops
  - Unclaimed students: Transported back to school, parents must collect within 1 hour

6. EMERGENCY RESPONSE
  6.1 Incident Protocol:
    a) Bus attendant immediately notifies school transport coordinator
    b) Transport coordinator activates Transport Emergency Response Team (TERT)
    c) School principal notified within 5 minutes
    d) Parents/guardians of affected student(s) notified within 30 minutes
    e) Local administration and emergency services (108/112) called as needed
    f) Incident documented on Form TR-01 (Transport Incident Report)
    g) TERT reviews and submits action plan within 48 hours

  6.2 Emergency Contact Numbers:
    - School Principal: ___________
    - Transport Coordinator: ___________
    - Police Control Room: 100
    - Ambulance: 108 / 112
    - Fire Service: 101

7. MONTHLY MAINTENANCE AND INSPECTION
  - Daily pre-trip inspection checklist (driver + attendant)
  - Weekly tire, brake, and light check
  - Monthly full vehicle inspection by authorized mechanic
  - Quarterly GPS and CCTV system audit
  - Annual comprehensive roadworthiness test

8. REVIEW AND COMPLIANCE
  - SOP reviewed quarterly by Transport Committee
  - Audit conducted every 6 months by external assessor
  - Non-compliance may result in vehicle de-registration from school fleet
  - Next review date: 01-Oct-2025
